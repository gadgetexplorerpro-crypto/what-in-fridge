Privacy Policy

What's in My Fridge? (Application ID: com.aistudio.whatsinmyfridge.qknz)

Effective Date: October 21, 2026 | Last Updated: October 21, 2026

Summary: What's in My Fridge? is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. Your pantry data, meal plans, and grocery lists are stored locally on your device. We do not sell your personal data, track your location, or use advertising identifiers. Camera and photo access is used strictly on demand when you scan food ingredients.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how What's in My Fridge? ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our Android mobile application. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. User-Provided Information (Stored Locally)

  • Kitchen Inventory: Names of ingredients, quantities, units, storage locations (fridge, freezer, pantry), and expiration dates that you manually add or confirm after scanning.
  • Dietary Preferences and Allergies: Dietary styles (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Keto) and allergen exclusions (e.g., Peanuts, Dairy, Gluten) selected to tailor recipe suggestions.
  • Meal Plans and Shopping Lists: Planned meals and shopping list items that you create or sync.

All of the above information is stored locally on your device in secure, app-private application storage.

B. Device Media & Camera Data

  • Camera & Photo Gallery Access: When you use the "AI Photo Ingredient Scanner" feature, the App requests access to capture or select a photo of your refrigerator shelves, countertops, or groceries.
  • Usage: The selected image is transmitted securely via HTTPS to Google's Gemini Vision API solely to identify visible food items. The image is processed ephemerally and is not retained, stored permanently, or used for facial recognition, biometric analysis, or user profiling.
  • Photo Picker: We implement the modern Android Photo Picker, allowing you to select only specific photos without granting broad storage or media access to your device.

3. Third-Party Services and AI Processing

To provide intelligent food identification and personalized recipe recommendations, the App integrates with:

  • Google Gemini AI (Google Cloud Platform): Used to analyze food images and generate recipes based on available ingredients and dietary restrictions. Google processes these requests in compliance with Google Cloud's enterprise security and privacy standards. For more information, visit Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or social media tracking SDKs.

4. Data Retention and Deletion (Your Rights)

  • Complete User Control: You can edit or delete individual ingredients, recipes, and shopping list items at any time within the App.
  • One-Tap Data Deletion: You can completely wipe all locally stored data, inventory, recipes, and preferences by navigating to Chef Profile & Settings > Data Management > Reset All Data to Defaults.
  • Uninstalling the App: Because data is stored in the application's private sandbox, uninstalling the App immediately removes all stored data from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for or directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information. All communications between the App and AI services are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Stored inventory is isolated within Android's sandboxed application data directory.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App and reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
