Privacy Policy

What's in My Fridge? (v4.0)
Last Updated: October 21, 2026

Key Privacy Summary: Your kitchen inventory, meal plans, and grocery lists are stored locally on your device. We do not sell your personal data, track your location, or use advertising identifiers. Camera access is used strictly on demand when you scan food ingredients.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how What's in My Fridge? ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our Android mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

A. Locally Stored Information

  • Kitchen Inventory: Ingredient names, quantities, units, categories, storage zones (fridge, freezer, pantry), and expiration dates you add or confirm.
  • Dietary Preferences: Diets (e.g. Vegetarian, Keto) and allergens (e.g. Peanuts, Gluten) chosen for customized recipes.
  • Meal Plans & Shopping Lists: Planned meals and shopping list items you create.

B. Camera & Media Usage

  • Photo Scanning: When you use the "Scan Ingredients" feature, you can capture or select a photo of your fridge or groceries.
  • Ephemeral Processing: Photos are sent securely via HTTPS to Google's Gemini Vision API solely to identify food items. They are never retained, stored permanently, or used for facial recognition or user profiling.
  • Android Photo Picker: We implement the modern Android Photo Picker which requires zero broad media storage permissions.

3. Third-Party Services

The App connects with Google Gemini AI to power smart recipe generation and ingredient detection. All requests are protected by Google Cloud enterprise security. No third-party ad networks or analytics tracking SDKs are included.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

  • You can edit or delete any inventory item or recipe at any time.
  • You can perform a complete data reset anytime via Settings > Data Management > Reset All Data to Defaults.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local data from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
